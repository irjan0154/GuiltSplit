Anti-Cheat Committee Ruling
Case reference: IP-S4-017
Tournament: IronPulse Season 4 Finals
Roster under review: Team Aurora (Kestrel, Quill, Ember, Drift, Sable)
On 2026-10-16 the committee reviewed the final-map footage and finds that Kestrel used a radar hack during the final map.
Teammates Quill, Ember, Drift and Sable saw the radar hack in use during the match and did not report it.
The committee finds the whole roster complicit.
