IronPulse Esports League (fictional league)
Anti-Cheat Committee Ruling
Case reference: IP-S4-017
Tournament: IronPulse Season 4 Finals
Roster under review: Team Aurora (Kestrel, Quill, Ember, Drift, Sable)
The committee finds that on 2026-08-04, during an online qualifier played well before the finals, Kestrel used a radar hack.
The committee found no evidence that Quill, Ember, Drift or Sable knew of the radar hack, and they are not sanctioned.
The violation is attributed to Kestrel alone.
This ruling is final for the purposes of prize distribution.
