IronPulse Esports League (fictional league)
Anti-Cheat Committee Ruling
Case reference: IP-S4-017
Tournament: IronPulse Season 4 Finals
Roster under review: Team Aurora (Kestrel, Quill, Ember, Drift, Sable)
On 2026-10-16 the committee reviewed the final-map footage after a complaint against Kestrel.
The committee finds that Kestrel did not use any prohibited software, and the complaint is dismissed.
No sanction is imposed on Kestrel or on any other member of the roster.
This ruling is final for the purposes of prize distribution.
